# Product Metrics

## Why Metrics Matter

For MessMate, I would use product metrics to understand whether students are actually using the feedback system and whether the feedback is useful.

## Main Metrics

### 1. Feedback Participation Rate

This shows how many students give feedback after eating a meal.

**Formula:**

Feedback Participation Rate = Students Giving Feedback / Students Who Ate the Meal × 100

### 2. Average Meal Rating

This shows the average rating received by a particular meal.

For example:

**Rajma → 4.1 / 5**

### 3. Meal-wise Rating

Instead of looking only at the overall rating, I would compare ratings for:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner

This can help identify which meal has lower ratings.

### 4. Repeat Usage

This measures how many students continue to use the feedback feature over time.

### 5. Written Feedback Rate

This shows how many students add comments along with their rating.

## Success Metrics

For the initial version, I would mainly track:

- Feedback participation rate
- Average meal rating
- Repeat usage
- Written feedback rate

These metrics can help understand whether the product is being used and whether students are providing useful feedback.

## Example

Suppose 300 students eat a particular meal and 120 students give feedback.

**Feedback Participation Rate = 120 / 300 × 100 = 40%**

This can then be compared with participation for other meals.

## Note

The numbers used in the examples are only illustrative and are not based on actual college data.
