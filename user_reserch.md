# User Research

## Why I Looked at This Problem

I wanted to understand how students experience mess food and what kind of problems they may face while giving feedback.

In a college with multiple hostels, the number of students eating in different messes can vary a lot. Some messes may serve around 500 students, while larger hostel messes can have more than 1,000 students.

Because of this, feedback from different messes may also be different.

## Problems I Considered

Some common problems I considered are:

- Students may not rate every meal
- Overall monthly ratings may not show meal-wise problems
- Lunch may be less preferred than other meals
- Students may have different food preferences
- Repeated complaints may be difficult to identify
- Feedback from different hostels may show different patterns

## User Groups

I considered three basic user groups:

### 1. Regular Student

A student who eats most meals in the mess and may regularly give feedback.

**Need:** A quick and simple way to rate food.

### 2. Occasional Feedback User

A student who eats in the mess but does not give feedback regularly.

**Need:** An easy feedback process that takes very little time.

### 3. Mess Management

The people responsible for understanding food quality and student feedback.

**Need:** Simple insights that show which meals or problems need attention.

## User Journey

The basic journey I considered is:

**Check Menu → Eat Meal → Rate Meal → Give Feedback → View/Analyse Insights**

The main opportunity is to make the feedback process simple and make the collected feedback more useful.

## Key Questions

Some questions I would like to understand through user research are:

- Which meal do students like the most?
- Which meal gets lower ratings?
- Why do students give a low rating?
- Does food preference differ between students?
- Do students give feedback regularly?
- Are there differences between different messes?
- What type of feedback is most useful for improving meals?

## Main Pain Points

Based on this problem, the main pain points I identified are:

1. Feedback may not be detailed enough.
2. Overall ratings may hide meal-specific problems.
3. Students may not give feedback regularly.
4. Different student preferences may not be considered.
5. It may be difficult to convert feedback into clear actions.

## Product Opportunity

The main opportunity is to make food feedback more structured.

Instead of only collecting a monthly rating, the product can collect meal-wise feedback and analyse it by meal, time and student preference.

This can help identify patterns and give more useful insights.

## Note

This is a personal product management project. The user research points are based on problem observation and product assumptions, not on a formal survey of college students.
